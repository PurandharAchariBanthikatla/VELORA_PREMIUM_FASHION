Shipping Policy

Last updated: 2026

1. Processing time

Orders are processed once payment is confirmed, usually within 1–2 business days before being handed to our shipping partner.

2. Shipping fees and free shipping

Shipping is calculated at checkout. Orders above the store's free-shipping threshold ship at no extra cost — the current threshold is shown in your cart and at checkout.

3. Delivery timelines

Typical delivery is 3–7 business days domestically, depending on your location. You can track progress at any time from My Account → My Orders, which shows a status timeline (confirmed → processing → shipped → delivered) for every order.

4. Order status & notifications

You'll get an email whenever your order's status changes, and every update also appears in your account's Notifications feed.

5. Address accuracy

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout — we ship to the address on the order and can't guarantee changes once an order has moved to "processing".

6. Lost or delayed shipments

If your order hasn't arrived within the expected window, contact support with your order number and we'll investigate with our shipping partner.

7. Contact us

Questions about a shipment can be sent to support@velora.com or via a support ticket from your account.
