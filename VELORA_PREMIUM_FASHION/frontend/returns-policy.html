Returns & Refunds Policy

Last updated: 2026

We want you to love what you ordered. If something isn't right, here's how returns work.

1. Return window

You can request a return within 7 days of your order being marked delivered. Requests submitted after this window can't be processed automatically — contact support and we'll review it case by case.

2. How to request a return

Sign in, go to My Account → My Orders, open the delivered order, and choose "Request Return" on the item you'd like to return. Tell us the reason and quantity. You'll see the request and its status under that order going forward.

3. Review and approval

Our team reviews each request and will approve, reject, or ask for more information. You'll get an email at each step. Approved returns are either refunded to your original payment method or resolved as an exchange, at our discretion and in line with what you requested.

4. Refunds

Approved refunds are issued to the original Stripe payment method. Refund timing depends on your bank/card issuer and typically takes 5–10 business days to appear once processed. You'll receive an email confirmation when a refund is issued.

5. Non-returnable items

  • Items marked "final sale" at the time of purchase.
  • Worn, washed, or altered items without original tags.
  • Gift cards.

6. Damaged or incorrect items

If an item arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, request a return and note this in the reason — these are prioritized and typically don't require the item to be sent back before a refund is issued.

7. Contact us

Still have questions? Reach us at support@velora.com or open a support ticket from your account.
